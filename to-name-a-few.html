Lucid Motors

Tahoe photo and live-action shoot, CGI backplates, drone photography

Executive produced photo, video, and CGI shoot across multiple Tahoe locations, coordinating California Highway Patrol road closures on steep hairpin turns while managing complex safety protocols for high-altitude vehicle filming. Led the asset capture strategy that created an extensive CGI backplate library and established production standards for the vehicle's first accessories launch.

The shoot's methodology and visual language became the template for all subsequent vehicle campaigns, including the $4.35M SUV Global Reveal. Strategic foresight in asset planning maximized ROI across multiple launches while setting the precedent for future high-stakes automotive content production.

Lucid Motors

360 campaign, Tahoe

Deliverables: photo and video (long-form and cutdowns), OOH, digital, social, an interactive website configurator, CRM nurture email campaigns, and experiential activations (the Dream Ahead road tour), reaching customers at every stage of the funnel.

The challenge: without a car on the road, we needed to turn a startup automotive vision into a premium global brand through storytelling that bridges engineering innovation and luxury lifestyle. We scaled from zero to building an agile in-house creative agency capable of producing CGI, video, photography, animation, and digital experiences that could adapt to rapidly evolving product specs, all while holding the integrity of the vehicle design.

I built systems that made exceptional work possible when faced with shifting engineering specs and compressed timelines. My philosophy: "it's all figureoutable."

  • Maintained harmony across competing cross-functional priorities

Slack

Go-to-market experiential space: programming, physical build and interior design, merchandising, digital content

The challenge: to create an inviting educational space that lets users experience Slack's capabilities firsthand. As Slack's first branded built environment showcasing its customer-centric approach, this project was nothing but ambiguity. Translating abstract software features into tangible interactions required reimagining how users could physically interact with a digital product while maintaining the warmth and functionality that defines Slack's brand.

My role: Senior Experiential Creative Producer, Creative Operations

As Slack's first experiential producer, I translated digital brand values into physical environments. I sought and secured internal partners for a project that demanded cross-functional contributions yet wasn't on any department's roadmap.

  • Bridged creative vision with technical implementation constraints
  • Adapted digital brand elements to three-dimensional spaces
  • Worked through ambiguity to create clear project frameworks
  • Balanced educational goals with community-building functions

Results:

  • Delivered a $7M customer education experiential space (3,000 sq. ft.) on time and within budget
  • Created a modular environment with 6 interactive digital content modules designed for intuitive product education
  • Established experiential design protocols that improved team efficiency
  • Completed the project in March 2020, just as pandemic restrictions prevented the public launch

Creative partners: OMFGCO (architecture and interior), R/GA (content), O+A (permitting and legal), SC Builders (general contractor).
